=== Explainable AI using SHAP (Transformer Encoder) ===

1. Why needed:
Neural networks are typically 'Black Boxes'. SHAP allows us to see exactly which features drove the carbon emission predictions up or down.

2. How used:
We used shap.GradientExplainer on the trained Transformer Encoder, comparing test samples 100-200 against a background baseline of the first 100 held-out test windows. Per-feature attributions are summed across the 7 time steps so that base_value + sum(SHAP) reconstructs the prediction.

3. Results (Top 3 Drivers):
  - Temperature
  - Industry Level
  - Energy Usage

4. Interpretation:
The model prediction is mainly influenced by Temperature and Industry Level.

5. Dataset Comparison:
  - Average Dataset Emission (Scaled baseline): 1.1587
  - Current Sample Emission (Scaled): 1.3282
